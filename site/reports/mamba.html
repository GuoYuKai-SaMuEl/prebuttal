AC estimate: 8/10

The submission introduces Mamba, an attention-free sequence modeling architecture built upon structured state space models (SSMs) augmented with an input-dependent selection mechanism and a hardware-aware parallel scan algorithm. All three reviewers (R1, R2, R3) find the paper to be exceptionally strong, highlighting the principled formulation of the selection mechanism, the clever hardware-aware GPU memory optimization that avoids materializing large expanded states, and comprehensive empirical validations across language, DNA, and audio modalities. Reviewers uniformly praised Mamba's ability to match or exceed Transformer performance (e.g., Mamba-2.8B outperforming Pythia-2.8B on zero-shot tasks in Table 3) while scaling linearly with sequence length and achieving up to 5× higher generation throughput (Figure 8). Substantive points raised include the "No Free Lunch" continuous-discrete spectrum—where the selection mechanism can degrade performance on continuous signals like audio waveforms unless restricted to LTI configurations (Figure 10)—and the lack of evaluation at the 7B+ parameter scale where modern open-source baselines are heavily tested. Additionally, R3 noted the absence of run-to-run variance or error bars across the empirical evaluations. However, these limitations do not detract from the core technical breakthrough. All reviewers recommend acceptance. The paper represents a major milestone in subquadratic sequence modeling.
