Answer Reviewer 2 before Reviewer 2 exists.

Prebuttal is a pre-submission stress test for ML papers: three AI reviewers with different temperaments — running in fully independent contexts — plus an area chair, delivering a conference-format review report before the real Reviewers 1–3 see your manuscript.

pipx install prebuttal
▶ See a sample report GitHub

Why not just paste the paper into a chatbot?

One conversation role-playing three reviewers collapses into one bland compromise opinion.

Three reviewers, zero shared context

Each reviewer is a separate call or process with its own persona, model and seed — verdicts genuinely differ, and the disagreement (σ) is reported, always.

Real review forms

NeurIPS 1–6, ICLR 1–10, ICML 1–10 and ARR Soundness/Excitement/Overall — with the official scale anchors, not invented ones.

Cell-accurate ingestion

LaTeX parsing, PDF table extraction with per-cell geometry, caption fallback — reviewers cite your “Table 3, column 4”, not a guess.

Honest by construction

Every review is labeled with its backend and model; a calibration script measures scores against real OpenReview ratings (results not yet published); run history diffs weaknesses across revisions.

Sample runs

Full runs on public arXiv papers, compared with their real OpenReview outcomes. Unofficial demos — not written or endorsed by the papers' authors.

GlobalGate: A Universal Attention Mechanism That Solves Long-Context Reasoning

Synthetic paper · full report →

What a weak submission gets. A fictional paper written for this demo with deliberate flaws — baseline trained 1 epoch vs. 5, hyperparameters tuned on the test set, one seed, no competing methods, "universal" claims from one 2k-token task. All three reviewers caught every flaw and cited the paper's own lines.

1/10
3 reviewers, unanimous
fictional · no real authors
Calibration caveats, stated plainly: frontier models have almost certainly seen these papers in training (contamination); the flash-lite panel skewed lenient on Mamba and produced no dissenter. These comparisons illustrate the workflow, not a validated predictor. A stress test, not a prophecy.
