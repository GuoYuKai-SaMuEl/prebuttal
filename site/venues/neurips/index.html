NeurIPS 2026 submission checklist

A desk-reject pre-flight for NeurIPS: the page limit, required sections, anonymity and leftovers — checked in seconds, with no model and no cost.

pip install prebuttal
prebuttal check paper.pdf --venue neurips       # the PDF you will upload
prebuttal check paper.zip --venue neurips       # Overleaf: Menu → Download → Source

What the check holds a NeurIPS paper to

  • Main text within 9 pages. The count stops at the first of References, Acknowledgments, NeurIPS Paper Checklist; two-column layouts are read column by column. Over the limit is a desk reject. Checked on the PDF (a LaTeX project needs compiling first).
  • A “NeurIPS Paper Checklist” section. The call requires it; the check looks for a heading with that name.
  • Anonymity (double-blind): no author name in the PDF metadata, no e-mail address on page 1, not the template's camera-ready or preprint build, no acknowledgements section, no links to your own repository, no “our previous work [12]”.
  • Leftovers: unresolved ?? references, [?] citations, TODO / FIXME, and text hidden from human readers (white or tiny text — venues have desk-rejected papers for hidden instructions to reviewers).

NeurIPS asks for its paper checklist after the references; papers without it can be desk-rejected.

Rules taken from https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers. They change every year — confirm against this year's call. Anything the check flags as an error makes prebuttal check exit with code 6, so it can fail a CI job (see the GitHub Action).

The NeurIPS review form

Besides the scores, every review answers:

  • Paper Summary
  • Strengths
  • Weaknesses
  • Questions to the Authors
  • Suggestions and Typos

Rating 1–6

1Trivial or wrong
2Reject
3Borderline reject
4Borderline accept
5Accept
6Strong accept

Confidence 1–5

1My best guess
2Willing to defend, but not sure
3Fairly confident
4Confident but not absolutely certain
5Absolutely certain

What will reviewers raise?

The checklist catches what gets a paper rejected unread. To see what reviewers will say about the paper itself, run three independent AI reviewers and an area chair on this form:

prebuttal run paper.pdf --venue neurips
