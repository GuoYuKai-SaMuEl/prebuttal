ICLR 2027 submission checklist

A desk-reject pre-flight for ICLR: the page limit, required sections, anonymity and leftovers — checked in seconds, with no model and no cost.

pip install prebuttal
prebuttal check paper.pdf --venue iclr       # the PDF you will upload
prebuttal check paper.zip --venue iclr       # Overleaf: Menu → Download → Source

What the check holds a ICLR paper to

  • Main text within 9 pages. The count stops at the first of References, Ethics Statement, Reproducibility Statement, Acknowledgments; two-column layouts are read column by column. Over the limit is a desk reject. Checked on the PDF (a LaTeX project needs compiling first).
  • Anonymity (double-blind): no author name in the PDF metadata, no e-mail address on page 1, not the template's camera-ready or preprint build, no acknowledgements section, no links to your own repository, no “our previous work [12]”.
  • Leftovers: unresolved ?? references, [?] citations, TODO / FIXME, and text hidden from human readers (white or tiny text — venues have desk-rejected papers for hidden instructions to reviewers).

ICLR reviews are public on OpenReview, and every ICLR review must comment on the paper's stated limitations.

Rules taken from https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines. They change every year — confirm against this year's call. Anything the check flags as an error makes prebuttal check exit with code 6, so it can fail a CI job (see the GitHub Action).

The ICLR review form

Besides the scores, every review answers:

  • Summary
  • Strengths
  • Weaknesses
  • Questions to the Authors
  • Limitations — every ICLR review must comment on the stated limitations

Rating 1–10

1Reject — technically flawed
3Reject — not good enough
5Marginally below acceptance threshold
6Marginally above acceptance threshold
8Accept — good paper
10Award quality — award candidate

Confidence 1–5

1My best guess
2Willing to defend, but not sure
3Fairly confident
4Confident but not absolutely certain
5Absolutely certain

What will reviewers raise?

The checklist catches what gets a paper rejected unread. To see what reviewers will say about the paper itself, run three independent AI reviewers and an area chair on this form:

prebuttal run paper.pdf --venue iclr
