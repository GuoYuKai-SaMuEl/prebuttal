# ACL Rolling Review (ARR) form

You are filling in the official ACL Rolling Review form for this submission.

## Form fields

- **Paper Summary**: summarize the claims and contributions in your own words (3–6 sentences).
- **Summary of Strengths**: bullet list.
- **Summary of Weaknesses**: bullet list.
- **Comments, Suggestions and Typos** (optional): minor fixes and presentation issues.
- **Soundness** (1–5, half-steps allowed):
  - 5: Excellent — one of the most thorough studies I have seen, given its type.
  - 4: Strong — sufficient support for all of its claims.
  - 3: Acceptable — sufficient support for its main claims.
  - 2: Poor — some main claims are not sufficiently supported.
  - 1: Major issues — not sufficiently thorough to warrant publication.
- **Excitement** (1–5, half-steps allowed):
  - 5: Highly exciting — I would champion this paper.
  - 4: Exciting — I would mention it to others.
  - 3: Interesting — I might mention parts of it to others.
  - 2: Potentially interesting — does not resonate with me but might with others.
  - 1: Not exciting — does not resonate with the *ACL community.
- **Overall Assessment** (1–5, half-steps allowed):
  - 5: Consider for award — top ~2.5%.
  - 4: Conference — could be accepted to an *ACL conference.
  - 3: Findings — could be accepted to Findings of the ACL.
  - 2: Resubmit next cycle — needs substantial revisions.
  - 1: Do not resubmit — has to be fully redone.
- **Reviewer Confidence** (1–5):
  - 5: Absolutely certain.
  - 4: Confident but not absolutely certain.
  - 3: Fairly confident.
  - 2: Willing to defend, but not sure.
  - 1: My best guess.
