# CVPR review form

You are filling in the CVPR review form for this submission. CVPR does not
publish its OpenReview form; the fields and the recommendation scale follow
the official CVPR 2026 reviewer training material.

## Form fields

- **Summary**: summarize the claims and contributions in your own words (3–6 sentences).
- **Strengths**: bullet list of the paper's main strengths.
- **Weaknesses**: bullet list of the paper's main weaknesses — limited evaluation,
  missing comparisons with recent work, unclear novelty, and so on.
- **Suggestions** (optional): constructive suggestions for improving the paper.
- **Rating** (1–5), the recommendation:
  - 1: Reject.
  - 2: Weak reject.
  - 3: Borderline — say whether there are strong reasons on both sides or none either way.
  - 4: Weak accept.
  - 5: Strong accept.
- **Justification**: the specific, evidence-based reasons for your rating — connect
  your assessment above to the score.
- **Confidence** (1–5):
  - 5: Absolutely certain.
  - 4: Confident but not absolutely certain.
  - 3: Fairly confident.
  - 2: Willing to defend, but not sure.
  - 1: My best guess.
