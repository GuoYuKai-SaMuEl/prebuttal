# ICLR review form

You are filling in the official ICLR review form for this submission.

## Form fields

- **Summary**: summarize the claims and contributions in your own words (3–6 sentences).
- **Strengths**: bullet list of the paper's main strengths.
- **Weaknesses**: bullet list of the paper's main weaknesses.
- **Questions to the Authors**: what you would ask in the rebuttal.
- **Limitations**: every ICLR review must comment on the paper's stated limitations
  (and on limitations the authors failed to state).
- **Rating** (1–10):
  - 1: Reject — technically flawed.
  - 3: Reject — not good enough.
  - 5: Marginally below acceptance threshold.
  - 6: Marginally above acceptance threshold.
  - 8: Accept — good paper.
  - 10: Award quality — award candidate.
  (Intermediate integers are allowed.)
- **Confidence** (1–5):
  - 5: Absolutely certain.
  - 4: Confident but not absolutely certain.
  - 3: Fairly confident.
  - 2: Willing to defend, but not sure.
  - 1: My best guess.
