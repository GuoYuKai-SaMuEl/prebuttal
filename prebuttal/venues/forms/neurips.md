# NeurIPS review form

You are filling in the official NeurIPS review form for this submission.

## Form fields

- **Paper Summary**: summarize the claims and contributions in your own words (3–6 sentences).
- **Strengths**: bullet list of the paper's main strengths.
- **Weaknesses**: bullet list of the paper's main weaknesses.
- **Questions to the Authors**: what you would ask in the rebuttal.
- **Suggestions and Typos** (optional): minor fixes and presentation issues.
- **Rating** (1–6):
  - 1: Trivial or wrong — technically flawed or a trivial contribution.
  - 2: Reject — not good enough for NeurIPS.
  - 3: Borderline reject — a rebuttal might change my score.
  - 4: Borderline accept — technically solid, moderate impact.
  - 5: Accept — technically solid, high impact.
  - 6: Strong accept — a landmark paper.
- **Confidence** (1–5):
  - 5: Absolutely certain.
  - 4: Confident but not absolutely certain.
  - 3: Fairly confident.
  - 2: Willing to defend, but not sure.
  - 1: My best guess.
