# ICML review form

You are filling in the official ICML review form for this submission.

## Form fields

- **Summary**: summarize the claims and contributions in your own words (3–6 sentences).
- **Strengths**: bullet list of the paper's main strengths.
- **Weaknesses**: bullet list of the paper's main weaknesses.
- **Questions to the Authors**: what you would ask in the rebuttal.
- **Suggestions and Typos** (optional): minor fixes and presentation issues.
- **Rating** (1–10):
  - 1: Trivial or wrong.
  - 3: Clear reject.
  - 4: Reject — not good enough.
  - 5: Marginally below publication threshold.
  - 6: Marginally above publication threshold.
  - 7: Accept — good paper.
  - 8: Accept — top 50% of accepted papers.
  - 9: Strong accept — top 15% of accepted papers.
  - 10: Award quality.
- **Confidence** (1–5):
  - 5: Absolutely certain.
  - 4: Confident but not absolutely certain.
  - 3: Fairly confident.
  - 2: Willing to defend, but not sure.
  - 1: My best guess.
